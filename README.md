## 📌 Overview

**FinHealth AI** is an AI-powered financial risk prevention platform developed on the **ServiceNow Platform** to help banks proactively identify customers heading toward financial distress before loan delinquency occurs.

Instead of reacting after EMI defaults, the solution predicts financial risk using AI-powered insights, generates early warnings, automates intervention workflows, and provides operational visibility through modern ServiceNow Workspaces and Dashboards.

This project demonstrates how ServiceNow can be used beyond ITSM to solve complex enterprise business problems within the Banking & Financial Services domain.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Financial institutions often identify customer financial stress only after missed EMIs or loan defaults, leading to:

- Increased Non-Performing Assets (NPA)
- Higher collection costs
- Poor customer retention
- Manual servicing efforts
- Delayed intervention

The goal of FinHealth AI is to shift from **Reactive Collections** to **Proactive Financial Risk Prevention**.

---

# 💡 Solution

FinHealth AI continuously evaluates customer financial health using AI-driven analysis and automatically orchestrates intervention workflows before delinquency occurs.

The platform provides:

- Financial Health Scoring
- Early Warning Detection
- AI-Powered Risk Prediction
- Debt Restructuring Recommendations
- Customer Engagement Automation
- Operational Dashboards
- Workspace-based Case Management
